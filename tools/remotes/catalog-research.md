# Remote catalog research

Catalog researched 2026-11-07; original service/assistant marks rechecked 2026-11-08. These are **11 front layouts**, not a claim that every TV in a year shipped with the same remote. The first two are the pilot. Reference photos/manual illustrations were inspected for layout only; none are included in the repository or to be used as output artwork.

`modelYears` is a conservative selection hint for the original TV generation, **not** the remote's full replacement-compatibility range. Magic remotes were bundled with selected TVs and sold separately for other compatible TVs. Standard remote part numbers can persist across generations and be superseded by visually different replacements. The IR dates below are representative original generations supported by the cited parts/model evidence; complete factory shipment ranges are not established. Do not present this catalog as a guaranteed year-to-remote lookup.

## Photo inspection notes and exact variants

Generation dates for the IR entries were cross-checked separately from replacement listings: [LG's dated 2013 LN5700 series sheet](https://www.lg.com/content/dam/channel/wcms/ca_en/support/products/documents/LN5700_2013%20LG%20HE%20Spec%20Sheet_ENG.pdf), [LG 42LN570S manual dated April 2013 (mirror)](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/385792/lg-42ln570s-42ln577s-32ln570r-42ln575s-42ln5758-32ln5707-42ln5778-42ln5708.html), [LG 49LH604V 2016 sheet (mirror)](https://manuzoid.com.de/manuals/O6meQ-LG%2049LH604V%20Datenblatt), and [LG's 2019 49UM7300 product record](https://www.lg.com/cac/products/MD06090978.page). A TV-series date plus a parts fitment is evidence for a representative generation, not proof of every regional box's contents.

- AN-MR600 is **2015**, not 2016; AN-MR650 is 2016; AN-MR650A is 2017. LG's dated sheets establish this directly. The MR600 US gallery has a white Home key, a mic below the ring, and CAPTION, not the later service-key layout.
- The AN-MR19BA US gallery has `−/LIST`, `QUICK ACCESS`, `AD/SAP`, a GUIDE key below-right of the ring, and a third white `MOVIES` shortcut bottom-left. European MR19 faces can instead have TEXT/T.OPT/MOVIES arrangements. The prompt fixes the US gallery face and does not mix these variants. The EU manual is a functional cross-check, not the label authority for the US prompt.
- AKB75095308: the Mandis PDF includes a large original on the **left** and a completely different universal replacement on the right. Only the original was studied. Its `LIVE ZOOM`, `T.OPT`, `MAGIC LINK`, `REC/*`, `LIVE TV`, alphabetic number legends and two white app keys distinguish it from AKB75675304. Generic marketplace listings often show a newer substitute under this part number.
- AKB75675304: the LG US original face has three shortcut keys (including Movies), `CC/SUB`, `FAV`, `GUIDE`, `SAP`, `−/LIST`, and no alphabetic keypad legends. Parts listings also cover 2020 UN and 2021 UP TVs, but replacement compatibility alone does not prove that this exact face shipped with all of them. The skeleton deliberately uses representative 2019 only.
- AKB74915324: the inspected specialist image is angled and watermarked. It is a layout reference only; artwork must be new, straight-on, without that watermark. `RECENT`, `LIVE MENU` and `Q.VIEW` remain distinct from later streaming shortcuts. The 2016 LH/UH generation is used rather than extending the selection hint to every 2017 compatible set.
- AKB73715601: the inspected original has a blue SMART key and MY APPS. Some sellers now ship substitutes or show a basic SETTINGS face under the same number. Use the linked original photo, not a substitute. Original shipment coverage outside representative 2013 LN-generation TVs remains uncertain.
- MR21GA and MR23GN are related shells, but their number-pad lower-left legends differ in the selected originals: MR21 has LIST primary/GUIDE secondary; MR23 has GUIDE primary/LIST secondary. This is a printed-face distinction, not a new industrial design. Their service/assistant blocks also differ: the selected MR21 face has Google Assistant dots and an Alexa symbol; MR23 has Sling and an alexa wordmark. Retain these differences.
- MR25 is not a sufficiently precise part name by itself. The selected **MR25GA** Colombian face has no number pad and is 41/174 ≈ 0.236 in front width/height. Other markets have numbered MR25 variants; do not blend them. Retain its white double-loop AI emblem and the printed `AI` beneath the key. Its top-right screen/question icon is a real key, not an input icon.

## Artwork policy and mapping follow-up

Per the owner's 2026-11-08 decision, retain **all original service and assistant artwork**, colours, positions and keycap shapes. Retain model identifiers where physically printed, the MR23 NFC emblem and the MR25 AI loop. Omit **only the standalone LG company logo/wordmark on the body**, leaving its area plain. The **LG Channels** service wordmark stays on its key. Do not convert original service keys to unprinted caps.

Each branded service key opens its own app via the current `app:<id or title match>` action; there is no user-assigned shortcut overlay. Six physical shortcut keys remain six. Assistant keys need their appropriate supported action or an explicit unsupported action during later mapping. This documentation change does not modify `remotes.json` or MR19's existing mapped keys. Other entries with empty `keys` and `[0, 2400]` remain preparation placeholders until their actual processed images are reviewed and mapped. If an image is regenerated, verify all existing rectangles against the replacement before accepting it.

## Remaining fidelity limits

The European IR reference listings establish the pictured English-legend faces, but not a unique destination country. Print colours are visually matched to reference photos, not measured ink specifications; the tiny stacked Prime Video mark on the MR19 is particularly low resolution in the LG gallery. Prompts constrain the inspected layouts, but cannot guarantee exact model recall or small-text accuracy from an image model. Compare every generated result (shared chat or optional download) against its linked original, especially tiny secondary legends, tactile dots, and the rocker/ring geometry. Pure-white source backgrounds are intentional for local extraction; enclosed white keycaps must survive extraction. Neither image processing nor upscaling can repair an invented key or incorrect print. The two pilot images must be reviewed before the remaining prompts are used.

### Notes and deviations from the requested descriptions

- **AKB73715603 differs from AKB73715601.** The two share a shell, but 601 is the smart face: Q.MENU / GUIDE / RATIO / INPUT, FAV / APP/* / MUTE, blue SMART and MY APPS, REC and LIVE TV. 603 is the non-smart face: SUBTITLE / AV MODE / RATIO / INPUT, FAV / GUIDE / MUTE, SETTINGS / up / Q.MENU, and SIMPLINK / AD / stop, with no blue key. Parts lists overlap heavily because both send the same IR codes, so a TV list alone does not tell the two faces apart.
- **AKB72914043 (item 7) partly matches the requested description.** It matches on the LIGHT key at top right, the turquoise/pale-blue rockers, and the 3D and MARK / FAV / DELETE keys between the rockers. LG's 2011 manual and the genuine photograph show **Premium / Home / Q.MENU**, not MENU / SMART / MY APPS. They also show **no NETFLIX / APP / amazon row**. No genuine 2011–2012 LG IR remote with a LIGHT key and a NETFLIX / amazon row was found. The searches covered the EU AKB72914xxx / AKB73615xxx / AKB73275xxx originals and the US AKB72914201 / 240 / 287 / 043, AKB73615313 / 315 and AKB73295502. The 2012 US AKB73615313 has SETTINGS / HOME / MY APPS and 3D, but no LIGHT or service keys. The prompt follows the verified original and does not invent a streaming row.
- **6710T00017V (item 8) is the closest original but differs from the brief in several ways.** It matches the silver body, teal POWER, XSTUDIO key and TIME SHIFT. The requested FCST / PSM / P.CHECK / SSM rows, round colour keys and a bottom AV1–RGB direct-input panel were not found on any original in the 6710V / 6710T / MKJ32022 ranges inspected. On this original, the keys under the slide cover are SIZE, POSITION, INDEX, TIME, REVEAL, HOLD, SLEEP, FAV, PIP, ARC and I/II, per the parts listing. The prompt shows the cover closed. If the owner has a photo of the exact grey remote they meant, its part number label should replace this choice.
- **The MR400 (item 9) has no colour keys and no TV-icon key.** LG's manual lists only power, wheel/navigation, BACK, SMART, voice, 3D, 123/Q.MENU, VOL, CH and MUTE. The four colour keys and the TV key in the brief belong to later Magic Remotes. The European original prints `P` and a volume wedge instead of `VOL` / `CH`. The prompt follows LG's VOL / CH manual drawing.
- Width ratios are measured from the reference photographs (bounding box ÷ height). They are approximate.
